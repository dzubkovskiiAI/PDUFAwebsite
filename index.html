Where dates come from. The FDA does not publish upcoming PDUFA dates, so every date here comes from the company that received it: SEC filings (8-Ks and exhibits), company press releases, and FDA pages. Catalyst calendars and trading sites are not used.

How much to trust a row. Verified: two separate company, SEC or FDA channels give the same date, or a person reviewed it. Single primary source: one such source. Unverified: only news or other secondary coverage. Verified here means the channels agree, not that the company's own statement is correct.

Limits. US FDA only. Companies often name only a quarter, and private companies may disclose nothing, so this list is incomplete. Dates can move; check the linked source before acting. Not investment, legal or medical advice.
